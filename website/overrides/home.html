Pipeline

  1. Weeks 3–4

    Detect

    YOLO11 character detector, adapted from MTH to Nôm.

  2. Weeks 5–7

    Recognize

    BTTR decodes each character as a tree of components, in both directions.

  3. Weeks 9–11

    Align

    Match predictions to line transcriptions with dynamic programming.

  4. Weeks 12–14

    Self-train

    Fine-tune on pseudo-labels weighted by alignment confidence.
