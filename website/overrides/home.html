The pipeline

Four stages, one system

The class moves through the stages together. Every team builds all of them on the whole corpus, so results are comparable at the final.

  1. Weeks 3–4

    Detect

    Train a single-class YOLO11 detector on MTH pages, adapt it to Nôm with Nakagawa, and measure it on NomNaOCR.

    Detection labs →
  2. Weeks 5–7

    Recognize

    Decompose characters into IDS, then decode the BTTR recognizer both ways, left-to-right and right-to-left.

    Recognition labs →
  3. Weeks 9–11

    Align

    Assign boxes to lines, then run a two-level dynamic program that matches predictions to the transcription at the IDS level.

    Alignment plan →
  4. Weeks 12–14

    Self-train

    Weight each pseudo-label by its alignment confidence, fine-tune, and evaluate against the binary-flag baseline.

    Pseudo-labeling plan →
