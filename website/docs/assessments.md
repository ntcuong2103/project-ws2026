# Assessments

| Assessment | When | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midterm presentation | Week 8 · **Tue 15 Dec 2026** | 20% |
| Final presentation, written report, code repository | Week 15 | 60% |
| Code and report | Week 15 | 10% |
| Weekly review sessions | Weeks 2–14 | 10% |

## Midterm (Week 8)

A **15-minute** team presentation, followed by questions. Submit your slides and the current
state of your team repository **before class**.

Cover:

- detection and recognition architecture choices;
- training and evaluation metrics (precision, recall, mAP for detection; decoder behaviour for
  recognition);
- error analysis with concrete examples;
- your plan for the alignment stage (Weeks 9–11).

Include one **team-membership slide** listing every member's role and contribution in Weeks 1–7.
Each member presents their own entry. Full rules: [midterm presentation rules](guides/midterm.md).

!!! note "Rubric"
    The grading rubric will be published here before the midterm.

## Final (Week 15)

A **20-minute** presentation with questions and a demo, plus a written report and your code
repository. It covers the complete pipeline: the alignment algorithm, confidence-weighted
pseudo-labeling results, evaluation against the binary-flag baseline (CER, coverage,
precision/recall by confidence band), and robustness findings.

## Weekly reviews

At the start of each session every group has 5 minutes to present the previous week's
deliverable: what worked, what failed, and what is blocking. Keep it short and show evidence
(numbers, figures, failing cases).
